## [Visual Contrastive Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21556v1)
**Score:** 0.829  |  **arXiv:** 2607.21556v1
**Length:** 15 pages

This paper introduces Visual Contrastive Self-Distillation (VCSD), a method for vision-language models that eliminates the need for external teachers, privileged answers, or extra metadata during training. It generates a self-teaching signal by contrasting a teacher model's token predictions on the original image against a content-erased image, sharpening the target distribution toward visually grounded tokens. Evaluated on Qwen-VL models up to 8B parameters, VCSD consistently improves benchmark accuracy (e.g., +3.75% aggregate gain on 8B) with zero added cost at inference time.
